Yesterday, my friend Martin Günther and I were given the opportunity to present
your workshop **"Collaborative Data Modeling: Discovering Domain Types Through
Linguistic Cues"** at [Virtual Domain-driven
design](https://virtualddd.com/sessions/collaborative-data-modeling-linguistic-cues/). We
had a lot of fun and are thankful for all the helpful feedback from the
participants.

Thanks so much to [Andrea Magnorsky](https://www.linkedin.com/in/magnorsky/) for
the invitation and helping us set everything up and to everyone who joined,
either in the session or live on YouTube. You can watch a [recording of the
whole workshop](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iHa6u4t-kOA) there as well.


If you want to learn more about *CoDaMo*, take a look at the repo on Codeberg,
where we're [hosting an alpha version of the
handbook](https://codeberg.org/margue/CoDaMo).

Also, thanks to [Active Group](https://active-group.de/de/) for giving me the
space to work on this project.
