My colleague at [Active Group](https://active-group.de), [Beat
Hagenlocher](https://beathagenlocher.com) prepared a workshop for BOB
Konf 2026. Unfortunately, he got sick and I got to fill in spontaneously.

In the interactive tutorial, we watched and discussed the evolution of a
[SolidJS](https://www.solidjs.com) frontend app from state springled all over
the place to a place where illegal states are unrepresentable, `reducers` make
state transitions explicit and side effects are properly encapsulated. Using the
approach we discuss, our core domain logic ends up completely independent of any
SolidJS specifics.

The descriptoin of the workshop and a link to the slides can be found at the
[BOB Konf 2026 page for our tutorial](https://bobkonf.de/2026/hagenlocher.html).
